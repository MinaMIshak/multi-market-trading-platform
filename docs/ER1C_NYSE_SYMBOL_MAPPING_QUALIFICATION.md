The 2022 notice says the filename contains the trade date and gives the pattern
`<Market>SymbolMapping_YYYYMMDD`. It describes files as available by 12 midnight
ET and directs proprietary-feed subscribers to start-of-day reference data. The
later specification documents the public FTP location and fields for symbol,
NYSE trading market, listed market, tape, round lot, price scale and system IDs.

For a retained daily edition, `Listed Market = N` could support an NYSE listing
fact within that file's scope. The reviewed static-file layout has no security-type
field needed to prove the frozen common-stock cohort. Its symbol index is not
demonstrated here as a permanent security identity through events, ticker changes
or reuse. The documents do not assert complete listed-security coverage, define
historical archive retention, or bind corrections and later withdrawals.

“By 12 midnight ET” is not an exact timestamp for any pilot file and does not say
whether a received edition was final or later corrected. Future acquisition must
record actual receipt, edition and correction lineage. Current documentation
cannot become historical `known_at` evidence.
