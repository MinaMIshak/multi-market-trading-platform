# ER1A — minimum authentic PIT data acquisition specification

Status: provider-neutral design and acceptance gate, not an acquired dataset or an empirical result. No fixture, current snapshot, adjusted series, or model validation is source evidence. This specifies the smallest coherent package for an initial retrospective run; it does not relax R1.1/R1.2, US1–US5B, US7B/US7C, M6–M8, or their fail-closed rules.

## Common acquisition envelope

Acquire immutable original bytes for each distinct source artifact, not just normalized rows. Record provider, source, locator, exact byte count and SHA256, actual local UTC receipt, nonempty source edition/version, evidence category, and raw row/record locator. Preserve corrected editions separately with new hashes. For every exact value/version consumed, supply a `HistoricalEvidencePackage`: source receipt, exact scope and covered fields, revision semantics, `EXACT` or inclusive `BOUNDED_INTERVAL` source availability, source-authority attachments with their hashes and actual receipt times, and an approved human review binding the same receipt and attachments. The latest possible availability must be at or before the simulated decision; receipt, attachment receipt, and review must be at or before research build. Historical date, file timestamp, current web page, and later local receipt do not prove historical availability. Use canonical `datetime.timezone.utc` clocks. Keep original bytes and review material available for independent audit; model hashes alone do not authenticate a source.

Preserve stable platform instrument IDs independently of mutable symbols, provider keys, and company names. Build a dated crosswalk with source identifiers and every ticker/name/listing change, reuse, delisting and removal. Complete exact-date universe snapshots must include then-members that are absent today and mark eligibility explicitly; a missing date cannot be forward-filled or interpreted as an empty universe. Obtain independent records showing the historical roster and its completeness, plus deletion/delisting histories and sample checks against original dated source editions. This is the minimum evidence for a survivorship-safety claim; a contemporary constituents download cannot establish it.

Declare the intended market, MIC, security type, cohort selection rule, interval, decision cutoff, data frequency, raw price basis, source editions, and exclusions before inspecting outcomes. Keep the recent frozen holdout identity and boundaries in a predeclared M8 protocol, separate from development and parameter selection. A holdout observed or tuned against is no longer untouched.

## EGX acquisition package

| Boundary | Minimum authentic input and admission condition |
| --- | --- |
| Daily prices | Original **raw, unadjusted** market-date OHLCV, stable `instrument_id`, exact-date canonical and provider symbols, source provider, source snapshot date, source row number and SHA256. Supply `semantic_class` and `quality_flags`; all five OHLCV values are mandatory for `VALID_EXECUTABLE`. `provider_adjusted_close_reference` may be absent and, if present, is audit-only. Retain anomalies as quarantined raw evidence, never repair them into executable bars. |
| Identity and universe | One evidenced `HistoricalIdentityMapping` for each target instrument on every trading date, including both sides of symbol changes. One complete `UniverseEvidence` for every trading session date with exact `effective_date`, `published_at`, all members' stable IDs, symbols and explicit eligibility. Include later delisted/removed instruments that met the declared historical cohort rule. |
| Calendar | One evidenced `HistoricalSessionRecord` for **every calendar date** in the requested interval, including non-sessions; explicit market and instrument states. `SUSPENDED` requires affirmative evidence. `UNSUPPORTED` cannot be admitted. Session hours must not be invented. |
| Actions | Complete bounded `ActionEvidence` per instrument across the whole requested interval, including explicit empty coverage. Preserve event ID, effective date, type, terms and details; splits require `new_shares` and `old_shares`. Acquire dividend, rights, capital changes, symbol changes, delistings and other events even when current R1.2 cannot transform them. A series crossing a relevant non-split event is not eligible for R1.2 split-only derivation. |
| Intraday and liquidity | For any execution claim, obtain genuine final, continuous, opening-origin timestamped OHLCV bars at the granularity accepted by the applicable EGX execution admission, with explicit session sequence, availability and provenance. Daily bars cannot stand in for fills, gaps, stops, MAE/MFE or participation. Daily and intraday volume, units, currency and any available turnover/trade-count or spread observations should be retained; missing optional liquidity measures remain declared unknown, while execution participation capacity needs actual bar volume. |
| Costs | Obtain dated EGX fee/commission schedule, taxes or levies where applicable, minimum/fixed and variable charges, and source support for chosen slippage and participation assumptions. Record effective dates and units; feed explicit M6 assumptions and sensitivity scenarios. Unknown cost inputs block an economic validation claim. |

Canonical EGX admission is `HistoricalDailyObservation(CanonicalDailyBar, HistoricalEvidencePackage)`, `HistoricalUniverseSnapshot(UniverseEvidence, HistoricalEvidencePackage)`, `HistoricalIdentityMapping`, `HistoricalSessionRecord`, and `HistoricalActionCoverage(ActionEvidence, HistoricalEvidencePackage)` within `HistoricalPITInput`, yielding `ResearchPITDaily`. Store source artifacts and evidence packages separately from derived rows; no operational M3 `PointInTimeDailyRepository` fallback. The acquisition package need not prescribe disk filenames or a vendor format; it must preserve enough exact fields and bytes to construct those canonical objects without invention.

## US acquisition package

| Boundary | Minimum authentic input and admission condition |
| --- | --- |
| Daily prices | Original `RAW_UNADJUSTED` market-date OHLCV for each eligible listing, stable instrument ID, canonical symbol, listing MIC, provider instrument key/symbol, source provider, raw row locator and SHA256. An adjusted close may be retained as audit reference only. An eligible open-session date requires a raw bar; sparse US3 history cannot justify absence. |
| Identity and universe | Exact-dated `USListingIdentity` with stable ID, symbol, listing MIC, security type, USD currency, provider key/symbol, source and primary-listing flag. Complete `USUniverseSnapshot` on every open or early-close date, with all members, stable IDs, MIC, security type and explicit eligibility. Preserve failed, merged, delisted and removed members and both sides of ticker changes and ticker reuse. Never infer intervening dates. |
| Calendar | `USSessionRecord` for **every calendar date** and each declared calendar MIC: `REGULAR`, `EARLY_CLOSE`, or `CLOSED`, `America/New_York`, and exact UTC open/close for open dates. Closed dates have no hours. Do not infer DST, holidays, early closes or MIC equivalence. |
| Actions | Complete `USCorporateActionCoverage` per stable instrument for the full requested interval; explicit empty coverage is valid negative evidence. Preserve splits, cash and stock dividends, mergers, spinoffs, symbol changes, delistings, rights and other events with event IDs and exact terms. Splits require share ratio; symbol changes require old/new symbols; cash dividends require USD amount. US5B only split-normalizes; an unsupported event crossed by the series fails closed. |
| Intraday and liquidity | For historical execution research acquire US7B `RAW_UNADJUSTED` M1 or M5 timestamped OHLCV, true session sequence, interval end and availability time, source row, segment bounds, coverage mode and original segment bytes. `FULL_SESSION` begins at explicit US2 open and ends at close; `BOUNDED_WINDOW` cannot masquerade as opening-origin replay. Preserve volume and available turnover, trades, bid/ask or spread information for liquidity assessment; bar volume and the participation assumption are mandatory for a capacity claim. US7B alone is not M6 executable; US7C eligibility and multi-session replay constraints still apply. |
| Costs | Acquire date-effective US commission/fee schedule and applicable exchange/regulatory charges, plus defensible spread/slippage and participation inputs. Record units, venue applicability and effective dates. M6 needs explicit entry, stop, target and scheduled-exit slippage bps, variable/fixed per-side costs and maximum volume participation when used; preserve source and sensitivity assumptions. |

Canonical US boundaries are independent `USListingIdentity` + evidence (US1), `USSessionRecord` + evidence (US2), raw daily fact + evidence (US3), `USCorporateActionCoverage` + evidence (US4), and `USUniverseSnapshot` + evidence (US5A), composed by US5B into `USRetrospectivePITDailyDataset`. US7B uses separate evidenced raw intraday coverage segments; US7C controls conversion to M6. Do not map US inputs into the EGX-specific daily semantic contract. Source bytes, evidence/review packages, canonical facts, PIT output, and execution artifacts remain distinct audit boundaries.

## Coverage and first pilot

Coverage is an acquisition requirement, not a claim that these dates are currently available. Before buying or downloading, freeze exact inclusive dates after checking genuine market calendar evidence and strategy lookback/label horizon. Acquire complete daily identity, calendar, universe, action and price dependencies for each chosen interval, plus sufficient **preceding** sessions for indicator warm-up and **following** observed execution/label horizon where the research protocol requires it. Do not let later observations enter an earlier decision.

| Purpose | EGX | US |
| --- | --- | --- |
| First pilot | One contiguous recent historical interval long enough for indicator warm-up, multiple signal decisions and completed outcome labels; exact dates predeclared. | Same, for one declared MIC and security type. |
| 2008 stress | Acquire only if authentic EGX source coverage and the declared instrument/cohort history actually reaches 2008; otherwise mark **not testable**, never proxy it with later data. | Acquire a contiguous 2007–2009 interval covering the 2008 crisis, with warm-up and post-decision labels. A newer-only cohort cannot support this test. |
| 2020 stress | Acquire a contiguous 2019–2021 interval spanning the 2020 disruption if the declared cohort existed; include explicit closure/suspension facts. | Acquire a contiguous 2019–2021 interval, including the 2020 crash and session changes. |
| Recent frozen holdout | Acquire a later, contiguous, predeclared interval after development; its outcome labels must mature before evaluation. | Same; do not inspect or select on its outcomes during development. |

The **minimum first PIT pilot cohort per market** is a predeclared, historically eligible stable instrument that spans the pilot interval, **plus at least one instrument historically eligible in that interval that later delisted, merged, failed or was removed**, if the declared universe had one. Include every member in the complete exact-date snapshots even if only those two are initially derived. Choose both by dated membership and status evidence, not by return or surviving price availability. If no later-removed member exists in that dated universe, document the source proof and expand the interval/cohort before claiming the pilot tested survivorship safety. At least one evidenced symbol-change/reuse case should be included when present; otherwise mark that branch untested rather than fabricate it. This pilot is for admission and bias checks, not statistical validation. The full M8 rolling/OOS and minimum-trade protocol is a separate larger acquisition gate.

## Pre-admission checks and rejection rules

1. Recompute original-byte hashes and sizes; verify row locators against bytes, immutable edition identity, source authority, scope, covered fields, review attachments and correction lineage. Independent review must attest that exact-date rosters include removed names and the source was not survivorship-filtered.
2. Re-admit each canonical evidence package against its decision/build clocks. Reject unknown edition, missing availability proof, bounded interval ending after decision, mismatched hash/scope/attachments, unapproved review, late build dependency, fabricated/backdated or unsupported source timing.
3. Check every requested calendar date and MIC; exact session, universe and identity coverage; unique stable IDs and same-date symbols; consistent universe/identity/bar listing; complete gap-free action coverage. Reject current-list projections, forward fill, inferred suspensions, missing eligible bars, unsupported sessions and ambiguous ticker reuse.
4. Check positive and coherent raw OHLC, nonnegative volume, correct raw basis, dated source/row provenance and duplicate/conflicting observations. Quarantine anomalies; never silently adjust, interpolate, sort around gaps or substitute adjusted close. Check split/action terms and US5B/R1.2 supported transformation boundaries.
5. For execution, check explicit final bar availability, full opening-origin chronology, contiguous intervals and sequences, session-grid alignment, volume participation and dated costs. Reject daily-to-intraday synthesis, partial windows presented as full sessions, multi-session plans unsupported by the relevant replay boundary, missing fee/slippage assumptions and economic claims based on gross P&L alone.
6. Freeze cohort, dates, strategy identity/version, M8 protocol, rolling folds and holdout before outcome inspection. Preserve rejected rows and reasons. Passing software validators is necessary but does not prove original source authenticity or a bias-free vendor database; human source review and retained raw evidence remain mandatory.

`UNKNOWN` is permissible only where a canonical field is optional and the unknown cannot change admission or economics: for example absent provider-adjusted close, optional spread/trade count, or an untested historical event branch clearly labeled as such. Unknown source edition, availability/revision timing, stable identity, exact-date membership/completeness, session state, eligible raw OHLCV, action coverage, required action terms, execution-bar volume/availability, or applicable economic inputs is **NO-GO** for the affected PIT/execution claim. An unsupported event or unavailable stress era is a declared coverage limit, never a synthetic substitute.

A provider-neutral package is accepted only when the original artifacts and authoritative timing/roster evidence support every required canonical field, independent review approves exact scope and editions, all relevant constructors/admission functions pass without relaxation, the pilot includes verifiable historical removals where applicable, and the resulting research artifacts retain their source/evidence identities. No vendor is selected by this specification. Until an authentic package passes these checks, first empirical runs for both markets remain **NO-GO**.
